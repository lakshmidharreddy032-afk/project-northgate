## Brand & Style

This design system reflects a high-velocity, forward-looking digital transformation and enterprise engineering consultancy. It balances institutional trustworthiness with technical audacity, speaking directly to C-suite technology buyers, engineering leaders, and enterprise architects.

The visual direction blends **Corporate / Modern** precision with **High-Contrast Electric Accents**. The core backdrop relies on deep, near-black obsidian foundations, illuminated by electric neon green and high-saturation digital violet. The aesthetic is clean, technical, and deliberate—eschewing frivolous ornamentation in favor of sharp hierarchy, purposeful whitespace, precise geometry, and high-legibility enterprise dashboards and case studies.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high contrast on dark foundations, reflecting digital engineering precision and next-generation cloud infrastructure:

- **Primary (`#02F576` - Electric Green):** Represents innovation, real-time feedback, operational efficiency, and forward motion. Used selectively for primary calls-to-action, key performance indicators, active states, and critical accent highlights.
- **Secondary (`#5F2CFF` - Electric Violet):** Represents enterprise intelligence, AI capabilities, and deep technical mastery. Serves as a complementary anchor for secondary interactions, high-level categorical tagging, and gradient illumination.
- **Neutral (`#0B0E14` - Obsidian Base):** The grounding dark canvas. Replaces sterile pure black with a rich, navy-tinted charcoal that reduces eye strain and provides atmospheric depth for data visualization and technical content.
- **Text Hierarchy:** Pure `#FFFFFF` for primary titles and active labels; `#9EABC0` for supporting descriptive copy and meta-text; `#64748B` for subdued hints, disabled states, and auxiliary table metadata.

## Typography

The type system is driven by **Montserrat**, providing geometric clarity, architectural confidence, and supreme digital readability.

- **Headlines & Display:** Heavy weights (`700`, `800`) paired with subtle negative letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) create high-impact editorial statements suited for enterprise value propositions and technical case summaries.
- **Body:** Standard weights (`400`) balanced with generous line heights ensure effortless scanning across multi-paragraph documentation, technical whitepapers, and operational metrics.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High-weight, uppercase or tracked-out small caps (`0.04em` to `0.08em` letter spacing) deliver instant recognition for status badges, metric units, category tags, and interactive control triggers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs a responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`1440px+` max canvas container width), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), and a 4-column layout on mobile (`< 768px`).

- **Grid Alignment:** Column gutters are set to `1.5rem` (`24px`) on desktop and scale down to `1rem` (`16px`) on mobile viewports.
- **Canvas Margins:** Section margins allow ample breathing space with `3rem` (`48px`) on desktop and `1.25rem` (`20px`) on mobile screens to preserve edge containment.
- **Component Rhythm:** Interior padding operates strictly on a base-4/base-8 modular scale: micro elements utilize `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px); form inputs, buttons, and navigation cells leverage `space-md` (16px); compound structural cards and modules scale into `space-lg` (24px) and `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined combination of **tonal layering** and **low-contrast technical outlines**, accented by subtle chromatic glows rather than traditional muddy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deepest background tone `#0B0E14`.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards / Panels):** Rendered in `#141923` with a crisp 1px border of `#283248` (30% opacity). Zero drop shadow in resting state.
- **Level 2 (Popovers / Dropdowns / Modals):** Rendered in `#1E2535` with a 1px border of `#283248` and an ambient, diffused shadow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Active / Focused Interactive Depth:** When cards or interactive panels receive focus or hover, they introduce a micro-glow powered by the brand accents: `0 0 20px -4px rgba(2, 245, 118, 0.15)` for primary interactions or `0 0 24px -4px rgba(95, 44, 255, 0.25)` for secondary intelligence modules.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** shape language to emphasize structural stability, engineering rigor, and crisp enterprise performance.

- **Standard Elements:** Inputs, buttons, segmented controls, and badges use `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii.
- **Containers & Cards:** Dashboard panels, enterprise metric cards, and modals use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- **Hero & Feature Media:** Large hero media blocks or featured banners utilize `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- Fully circular treatments (`9999px`) are restricted strictly to status pips, avatars, and specific floating notification indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#02F576` with `#0B0E14` bold text. On hover, background shifts slightly in luminosity with an electric green atmospheric micro-glow (`rgba(2, 245, 118, 0.3)`). Focus ring: 2px offset border in `#02F576`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with a 1px border in `#5F2CFF` and `#FFFFFF` text. On hover, fills with `rgba(95, 44, 255, 0.15)` and border transitions to full `#5F2CFF`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border or surface fill. Pure `#9EABC0` text, transitioning to `#FFFFFF` with an underlying hairline underline indicator on hover.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Surface background of `#141923` with a subtle 1px border in `#283248`. 
- Placeholder text in `#64748B`. 
- Active typing text in `#FFFFFF`.
- On focus, the border transitions sharply to `#02F576` accompanied by a 1px inner ring shadow, ensuring unambiguous accessibility. Validation errors use standard high-vis red (`#FF4D4F`) with an accompanying contextual label.

### Cards & Metric Panels
- Constructed on `#141923` with a 1px perimeter stroke of `#283248`.
- Metric panels feature oversized Montserrat numerical readouts (`headline-lg`) paired with micro tracking labels (`label-sm`).
- Hover states subtly elevate the card border from `#283248` to `#3B4866` with a smooth 150ms transition.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact padding (`0.25rem 0.75rem`) with `0.25rem` radius.
- Status/Technology Tags use a dark-tinted fill (`rgba(95, 44, 255, 0.12)`) paired with `#5F2CFF` text and a hairline border.
- Active/Live state badges use `rgba(2, 245, 118, 0.12)` paired with `#02F576` text and a pulsing circular indicator.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkbox squares (18x18px) and Radio circles (18px) featuring a dark `#141923` fill and `#283248` stroke.
- Checked state fills completely with `#02F576`, carrying an `#0B0E14` bold SVG checkmark or center pip for maximum contrast and instantaneous state verification.

### Tables & Data Grids
- Designed for high information density. Headers feature uppercase `label-md` in `#9EABC0` with a 1px dividing border.
- Rows use subtle zebra hover effects (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`), with numerical data right-aligned and monospaced or tabular-figured for instant comparison.
